# Listening checklist (P05)

Fill this in by ear. Files are in `~/sonari-data/derived/tts/`. Use headphones, and
play each file at least twice before scoring it.

Scale for every cell: `Y` = fine, `?` = borderline, `N` = a problem, `-` = not
applicable. Put the word or time of any problem in Notes.

## What to listen for

- **Intelligibility**: could a B1 learner write down what was said?
- **Final consonants audible**: the ending sounds must be heard, not swallowed.
  Sentence: plants, end, buds, shoots. Dialogue: important, heard, budget, cuts, Mark,
  that's, knows, goes.
- **Unnatural stress**: wrong word or syllable stressed, flat or sing-song rhythm.
  Watch "Accounting", "important", "Studio", "everything".
- **Speed artifacts** (0.8x mainly): buzzing, stretched vowels, gaps inside words,
  robotic pauses.
